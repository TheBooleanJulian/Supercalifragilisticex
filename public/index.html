Private Telegram Bot

Supercalifragilisticex

Turns a text message or a photo of an invite, flyer, or screenshot into a Google Calendar event — and DMs a morning brief of today's schedule.

  • 📸 Extracts events from photos and free text (Gemini Flash-Lite, with Claude Haiku fallback)
  • ✏️ Shows extracted details and lets you edit before anything is created
  • 📅 Creates events directly in Google Calendar via OAuth
  • ☀️ Sends a daily morning brief of the day's events, and answers /today on demand
View on GitHub

This bot is restricted to an allowlist of Telegram user IDs — it isn't open for public sign-up.
